# Terminal Hour — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

Terminal Hour is a clock app (alarms, world clock, stopwatch, timer, pomodoro and home-screen widgets).

**Terminal Hour does not collect, store off-device, sell or share any personal data.**

- The app has no internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
- No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs are included.
- Your alarms, cities, timer presets, pomodoro settings and preferences are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

**Permissions and why they are used**

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) | Ring alarms, timers and pomodoro phase ends at the exact time |
| Notifications | Show running stopwatch/timer/pomodoro and alarm alerts |
| Full-screen notifications | Show the alarm screen over the lock screen |
| Vibrate, wake lock | Vibrate and wake the screen when an alarm rings |
| Run at startup | Restore your alarms after the phone restarts |

**Children.** The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
